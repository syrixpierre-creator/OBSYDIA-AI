  1. Open Google and search for the company name. The official site is the first result: syrix-vision.xyz.

  2. On the home page, tap Join the Community. You can also use Join Us from the menu.

    syrix-vision.xyz ↗

  3. The recruitment page is free and open. Scroll down and pick the category that fits you: Developer, Marketing or Investor / Manager.

  4. Enter your full name, your Gmail address and your country, then answer the short questions for your track — level, a project link, experience and availability.

  5. Tell us why you chose SYRIX VISION, tick the data processing notice, then tap Submit application.

  6. The team reviews every application. If yours is approved, you'll receive a confirmation email at the Gmail address you entered.
