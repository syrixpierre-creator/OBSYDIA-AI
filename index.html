01 About

I build tools people keep using — clean APIs, honest interfaces, and code that still reads well six months later.

I'm Dawens, known online as Inconnu boy sensei — an open source full stack developer based in Maringá, Brazil. Most of my work lives in the open on GitHub, where I build web and Android apps — including SKYHOST, a hosting platform — and collaborate with other developers.

I'm also the CEO & co-founder of SYRIX VISION COMPANY, a small team shipping open source software together. I publish tutorials and build logs on YouTube and TikTok.
